1️⃣ Understanding Focus Bear’s Mission & Vision

📌 Explore the Focus Bear website to learn about the company’s mission, vision, and key features.

**Mission:**
Focus Bear's mission is to help people stay focused and reduce distractions by providing practical tools that support productivity, particularly for individuals with ADHD and busy lifestyles. The company aims to create a gentle and supportive productivity experience rather than relying on strict or overwhelming methods.

**Vision:**
Focus Bear's vision is to make it easier for people to build healthy routines, stay focused, and complete meaningful work across all their devices. The platform is designed to support users by encouraging sustainable productivity habits while reducing digital distractions.

**Key Features:**
- Cross-device distraction blocking that blocks distracting websites and apps on Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android.
- Morning and evening routines that guide users through step-by-step routines to build consistent habits.
- AI distraction detection that gently reminds users to return to their task without tracking keystrokes.
- Productivity-focused breaks that encourage stretching, movement, and short breaks to prevent burnout.
- Zen Mode that limits access to unnecessary apps and notifications during focus sessions.
- Emergency override ("Break Glass") feature that allows users to bypass blocks when genuinely necessary.

Overall, Focus Bear focuses on helping users improve concentration, develop healthy routines, and maintain productivity through supportive features designed around real user experiences.
