✅ Task: Compare Focus Bear with Other Productivity Apps

📌 Be completely honest in your evaluation—Focus Bear might not always be better than other tools, and that’s okay! The goal is to understand the competitive landscape and think critically about where Focus Bear stands.

📌 Research other productivity and focus apps such as:

1. Freedom - A distraction-blocking app designed to improve focus. It can block websites, apps, or even the entire internet across multiple devices. It also supports custom blocklists, focus sounds, and, on Premium, scheduled/recurring sessions and Locked Mode.

2. Cold Turkey - A stricter distraction blocker that can restrict websites, applications, games, and even the computer itself. It offers scheduled blocks, allowances/breaks, usage statistics, and locking options designed to make blocks difficult to bypass.

3. Tiimo - A visual planning and productivity app that combines calendars, to-do lists, routines, reminders, and focus tools. It includes visual timers and flexible scheduling, and is designed with neurodivergent users in mind while being usable by anyone who benefits from visual structure.

4. Routinery - A routine and habit-focused productivity app. Its main feature is a step-by-step routine timer that guides users from one activity to the next. It also includes routine tracking and tools for building consistent daily routines.


📌 Then, reflect on the following questions:

1. What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?
- Focus Bear is different because it combines several productivity techniques into one application rather than focusing on only one area. Apps such as Freedom and Cold Turkey mainly focus on blocking distractions, while Tiimo and Routinery focus more on planning and routines. Focus Bear combines distraction blocking, focus sessions, habit and routine building, and productivity support. This makes it more of an all-in-one productivity tool designed to help users stay focused while also developing better daily habits.

2. If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors?
- If I were a user, I would choose Focus Bear because it combines multiple productivity features in one place. Instead of using separate apps for blocking distractions, managing routines, and staying focused, Focus Bear provides these tools together. This could make it easier to manage my time, reduce distractions, build better habits, and stay productive throughout the day.

3. What’s one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn’t?
- One feature that Focus Bear could improve on is detailed visual planning, similar to Tiimo. Tiimo provides a visual daily planner that clearly displays tasks and routines in a structured timeline. Adding more advanced visual planning features to Focus Bear could help users organise their day and see upcoming tasks more easily.

4. Based on your research, what’s one improvement you think Focus Bear could make?
- One improvement Focus Bear could make is adding a more detailed visual daily planner, similar to Tiimo. This could allow users to clearly see their tasks, routines, focus sessions, and breaks in a visual timeline.
